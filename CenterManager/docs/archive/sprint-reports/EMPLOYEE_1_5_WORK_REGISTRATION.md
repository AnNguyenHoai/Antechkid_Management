# EMPLOYEE 1.5 — Monthly Work Registration

Business contract:

```text
Employee availability for next month
        ↓
Manager reviews availability
        ↓
Manager plans official schedule
        ↓
Attendance records actual work
```

Work Registration is not Attendance and does not create working-time entries.

## Roles

All employee-linked accounts can maintain their own registration. Admin/Manager can review all registrations and close the registration month after planning.

## Lifecycle

`DRAFT → SUBMITTED → CLOSED`

There is intentionally no approval/rejection workflow in this task: registration is planning input, not an approval request.
